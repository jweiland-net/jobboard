Alternative rendering to Types/List: renders each job as a card of a grid with a teaser of the description. Render it in Partials/JobList.html instead of Types/List via template override. The markup is the same for every visitor (List is cached): remember-jobs.js adds "highlight-row" to remembered cards via data-jobboard-uid.
  • {job.jobRole.title}

    {job.title}

    {f:translate(key: 'job.applicationDeadline')}: {job.applicationDeadline -> f:format.date(format: '{f:translate(key: \'dateFormat\')}')}

    {job.description -> f:format.crop(maxCharacters: 240)}
    The visually hidden job title makes "Read more" unique for screen readers. {f:translate(key: 'job.readMore')}: {job.title} {f:translate(key: 'job.readMore')}: {job.title} {f:translate(key: 'job.applyOnline')}
