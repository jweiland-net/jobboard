Default rendering of the job list: one card per job, readable on any width. The whole card links to the job (stretched link), the remember toggle sits above that link. objects: jobs to render. rememberedJobs (optional): UIDs of the jobs remembered by the visitor as keys, renders their checkboxes checked. Pass it from uncached actions only (rememberedJobs). On List and Search remember-jobs.js checks the boxes and adds "highlight-row" after asking RememberJobMiddleware. data-jobboard-remember-url: endpoint of remember-jobs.js.
