Info window of a job location (marker) on the map of the optional EXT:maps2. Rendered by RenderJobLocationInfoWindowContentEventListener in a middleware: there is no Extbase request. Use "extensionName" for f:translate and the prepared URIs of the job links instead of f:link.action. It is placed into the popup of the map library outside of ".tx-jobboard", the stylesheet styles ".jobboard-info-window" for that. Variables: jobLocation (JWeiland\Jobboard\Domain\Model\JobLocation)
